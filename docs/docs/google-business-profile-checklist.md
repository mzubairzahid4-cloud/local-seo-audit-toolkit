# Google Business Profile Audit Checklist

A practical checklist for reviewing and optimizing a Google Business Profile (GBP).

## 1. Basic Business Information

- [ ] Business name accurately represents the real-world business.
- [ ] Business name follows Google's guidelines.
- [ ] Primary business category is relevant and accurate.
- [ ] Additional categories are relevant to the business.
- [ ] Business address is accurate, if displayed.
- [ ] Service areas are configured correctly, if applicable.
- [ ] Phone number is correct.
- [ ] Website URL points to the correct website.
- [ ] Business hours are accurate.
- [ ] Special hours are updated when needed.

## 2. Business Description

- [ ] Description accurately represents the business.
- [ ] Main services are clearly explained.
- [ ] Description is helpful to potential customers.
- [ ] Content avoids unnecessary keyword repetition.
- [ ] Description follows Google's content guidelines.

## 3. Services and Products

- [ ] Relevant services are listed.
- [ ] Service names are accurate and understandable.
- [ ] Service descriptions are helpful.
- [ ] Relevant products are added, where supported.
- [ ] Prices are accurate, where applicable.
- [ ] Outdated services or products are removed.

## 4. Photos and Videos

- [ ] Profile has genuine business photos.
- [ ] Logo is accurate and recognizable.
- [ ] Cover photo represents the business.
- [ ] Photos reflect the actual location, team, or services.
- [ ] Images are clear and relevant.
- [ ] Outdated images are reviewed.

## 5. Reviews and Reputation

- [ ] Recent customer reviews are monitored.
- [ ] Reviews receive professional responses where appropriate.
- [ ] Responses are helpful and respectful.
- [ ] Review requests follow Google's policies.
- [ ] No incentives are offered in exchange for reviews.
- [ ] Suspicious reviews are reviewed for policy violations.

## 6. Google Business Profile Posts

- [ ] Relevant updates are published when useful.
- [ ] Posts contain accurate information.
- [ ] Offers include correct dates and details.
- [ ] Images are relevant to the post.
- [ ] Calls to action work correctly.
- [ ] Content is free from misleading claims.

## 7. Profile Accuracy and Maintenance

- [ ] Business information is reviewed periodically.
- [ ] Changes in operating hours are updated.
- [ ] Website links are tested.
- [ ] Customer questions are monitored where available.
- [ ] Duplicate profiles are investigated.
- [ ] Profile complies with Google Business Profile guidelines.
