# Local SEO Audit Checklist

A practical checklist for reviewing the Local SEO fundamentals of a local business.

**Purpose:** Identify missing information, technical issues, and opportunities to improve a business's local search presence.

**How to use:** Review each item and mark it as completed, needs improvement, or not applicable.

## 1. Google Business Profile (GBP)

- [ ] Business Profile exists on Google.
- [ ] Business ownership is verified.
- [ ] Business name follows Google's guidelines.
- [ ] Primary business category is accurate.
- [ ] Relevant additional categories are selected.
- [ ] Business address is accurate, if publicly displayed.
- [ ] Phone number is correct.
- [ ] Website URL is correct.
- [ ] Business hours are accurate.
- [ ] Special and holiday hours are updated.
- [ ] Business description accurately describes the business.
- [ ] Relevant services or products are added.
- [ ] Business photos are relevant and genuine.
- [ ] Customer reviews are monitored.
- [ ] Reviews receive helpful and professional responses.

## 2. Website On-Page SEO

- [ ] Website has a clear homepage.
- [ ] Homepage title tag describes the business and its service.
- [ ] Meta description accurately summarizes the page.
- [ ] Main heading (H1) describes the page topic.
- [ ] Relevant local keywords are used naturally.
- [ ] Business name, address, and phone number are accurate.
- [ ] Contact page is available.
- [ ] Services have relevant website pages.
- [ ] Images have descriptive alt text where appropriate.
- [ ] Internal links connect relevant pages.
- [ ] Website is mobile-friendly.

## 3. Local Business Information (NAP)

NAP means Name, Address, and Phone Number.

- [ ] Business name is consistent across important online listings.
- [ ] Business address is accurate wherever published.
- [ ] Phone number is consistent.
- [ ] Website contact details match the business's actual information.
- [ ] Incorrect or outdated listings are identified.
- [ ] Business location details are checked for accuracy.

Note: Consistent business information helps customers identify and contact the correct business. Do not assume every minor directory inconsistency is a direct Google ranking factor.

## 4. Local Keyword Research

- [ ] Main business service keywords are identified.
- [ ] Relevant location-based keywords are researched.
- [ ] Customer search intent is considered.
- [ ] Relevant service and location combinations are reviewed.
- [ ] Keyword opportunities are mapped to relevant website pages.
- [ ] Keyword usage sounds natural and helpful.

Example:

Business: Dental Clinic

Location: Lahore

Possible keyword: dentist in Lahore

## 5. Technical SEO Basics

- [ ] Website uses HTTPS.
- [ ] Important pages can be accessed by search engines.
- [ ] Important pages are not accidentally blocked by robots.txt.
- [ ] Important pages do not have unintended noindex directives.
- [ ] XML sitemap is available where appropriate.
- [ ] Broken links are identified.
- [ ] Website loading performance is reviewed.
- [ ] Mobile usability is checked.
- [ ] Relevant LocalBusiness structured data is reviewed where appropriate.

## 6. Reviews and Online Reputation

- [ ] Customer reviews are monitored.
- [ ] Negative feedback is handled professionally.
- [ ] Responses are helpful and relevant.
- [ ] Customers are not offered incentives for reviews.
- [ ] Review requests follow Google's policies.
- [ ] Fake or suspicious reviews are documented for appropriate reporting.
